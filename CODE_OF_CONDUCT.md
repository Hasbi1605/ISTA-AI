# Code of Conduct

ISTA AI is maintained as a respectful open-source project. Contributors, users, and maintainers are expected to keep discussions constructive, focused, and safe for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and patient in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy and never post secrets, private documents, personal data, or production credentials.
- Assume good intent, but accept maintainer decisions when scope or safety requires limits.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing exploit details, credentials, or sensitive data publicly.
- Repeated off-topic comments after maintainers ask to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block users; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.

For security-sensitive issues, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of public issue threads.
